# Safety And Copyright

## Universal evidence rule

Separate `provided`, `verified`, `inferred`, and `unknown`. Never convert an inference into a fact. Preserve URLs for claims a reader may act on.

## Strict scenes

### Health and fitness

- Do not diagnose, prescribe, promise outcomes, or advise stopping professional treatment.
- Flag red-flag symptoms for professional or emergency care.
- Treat supplements, rapid-loss plans, and contraindications as high risk; require credible evidence and individual caveats.

### Beauty and skincare

- Do not promise cures or universal compatibility.
- Include patch-test, allergy, pregnancy, medication, and professional-care caveats when relevant.
- Do not fabricate skin-test images or before/after results.

### Parenting

- Require age and context for safety-sensitive advice.
- Do not give unsafe sleep, feeding, medication, restraint, or developmental claims.
- Avoid shame, fear, and absolute milestones. Recommend professional care when signs are concerning.

### Personal finance

- Do not promise returns, imply guaranteed savings, or present sponsored claims as independent review.
- State assumptions, fees, time horizon, liquidity, and downside where relevant.
- Treat content as education and decision support, not personalized investment advice.

## Copyright and platform integrity

- Do not reproduce a third-party post, caption, image set, screenshot, template, or distinctive composition.
- Keep only public metadata, source links, short labels, and independently written pattern analysis.
- Attribute external facts and visuals. Prefer user-owned, licensed, public-domain, or self-created assets.
- Do not use platform logos or creator names in a way that implies endorsement.
- `xhs-hot-skill` is not affiliated with Xiaohongshu, Khazix, or Guizang.
- `guizang-social-card-skill` remains a separate AGPL-3.0 dependency. Do not copy its code, templates, or assets into this MIT project.
