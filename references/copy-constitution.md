# Copy Constitution

## Voice

- Name the subject. Prefer `我测试了...`, `这套方法适合...`, `读者可以...` over subjectless slogans.
- Use short, concrete sentences. One sentence should carry one claim.
- Prefer verbs such as `看、选、试、比、记、查、避开`; avoid `赋能、重塑、全方位、降维打击、效率革命`.
- Explain a term at first use or replace it with ordinary language.
- Keep confidence proportional to evidence. Say `更适合` instead of `最好`.

## Structure

1. Open with a specific situation, mistake, or decision.
2. State the promised result without inflated certainty.
3. Organize the middle around steps, criteria, route, or comparison.
4. Add one limitation or unsuitable case.
5. End with one concrete question. Do not stack calls to like, save, follow, and comment.

## Length

- Title: ideally 12-22 Chinese characters; clarity beats a forced number.
- Carousel: 5-9 pages, one job per page, usually 18-70 Chinese characters per page.
- Body: 300-500 visible non-whitespace characters. Exclude title alternatives, carousel copy, commands, sources, risks, and tags.
- Tags: 4-8, mixing scene, audience, problem, and format; do not stuff unrelated traffic terms.

## Anti-slop pass

Before delivery, remove:

- throat-clearing such as `在这个快速发展的时代`;
- repeated conclusions in different words;
- unsupported superlatives or numeric performance claims;
- fake quotations, fake personal use, and fabricated before/after results;
- dense emoji decoration. Use at most one functional symbol in a title and none when the visual system already carries emphasis.

Read the chosen scene recipe for its title formulas, rhythm, and examples. Do not copy sample titles verbatim; transfer the mechanism.
