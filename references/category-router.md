## Tie-breakers

- AI used to write meeting notes: route to `office-productivity`; AI itself being compared: `ai-tools`.
- A camera setup tutorial: `digital-software`; composition and shooting: `content-photography`; a specific camera review: `product-reviews`.
- A restaurant itinerary across a city: `travel-guides`; whether one restaurant is worth visiting: `local-discovery`.
- A product list driven by budget allocation: `personal-finance`; experience and performance comparison: `product-reviews`.
- Weight management through exercise: `fitness-fat-loss`; symptoms, treatment, supplements, or diagnoses: `health-wellness` and apply the strict safety gate.

For a multi-scene topic, choose one primary scene and borrow at most one supporting pattern. Do not merge whole recipes.
