# Output Contract

After the user chooses a direction, deliver every section below.

## 1. Positioning

- Primary scene and target reader
- Selected angle and one-sentence promise
- Known facts, assumptions, and missing materials

## 2. Title alternatives

Provide 4-6 titles with at least three mechanisms: direct result, contrast or mistake, and specific scenario. Do not change only punctuation or numbers.

## 3. Carousel copy

Provide 5-9 numbered pages. For each page specify:

- page job;
- headline and supporting copy;
- visual subject and layout;
- asset source or missing-asset fallback.

Keep one main idea per page. The final page should summarize an action or decision, not repeat the cover.

## 4. Body

Write 300-500 visible non-whitespace characters. Use explicit subjects, short paragraphs, one honest limitation, and no unsupported result claims.

## 5. Action kit

Include any prompt, checklist, route, recipe, or comparison criteria promised by the post. Make it directly usable.

## 6. Publishing details

- 4-8 relevant tags
- one interaction question
- suggested cover title and first image
- sources and licensing notes
- safety, factual, sponsorship, or freshness risks

## 7. Render result

When Guizang is available, give output paths and validator result. When unavailable, say `未渲染：缺少 guizang-social-card-skill` and include the render-ready handoff brief.
